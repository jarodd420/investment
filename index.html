    Company fundamentals

    Three points on the business

    Broadcom designs semiconductors and infrastructure software. These points use the company’s own third-quarter fiscal 2026 results, for the quarter ended August 2, 2026, reported September 2, 2026. They do not use the share price, a chart, or the analyst target.

    1. 1

      It designs custom AI accelerators and the networking around them

      Management said demand for custom AI accelerators and networking was very strong. AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7 billion in the quarter, up 221% from a year earlier and 54% from the prior quarter. The company guided AI semiconductor revenue to $21.7 billion in the fourth quarter, up 236% from a year earlier. That fourth-quarter figure is guidance, not a result.

    2. 2

      The semiconductor business is most of the company, and it already produces cash

      Semiconductor solutions were $20.8 billion, 70% of the quarter’s $29.6 billion of revenue, up 127% from a year earlier. GAAP operating income was $16.0 billion. Free cash flow was $13.7 billion, 46% of revenue, after $0.5 billion of capital spending. The case is the profit and cash the chip business already earns.

    3. 3

      Infrastructure software is a second franchise, not a side project

      Infrastructure software was $8.8 billion, 30% of revenue, up 29% from a year earlier. The company’s filings describe this alongside its data-center virtualization products, the business it expanded by acquiring VMware. In the same quarter the chip line surged, the software line still grew.

    Figures are from Broadcom’s September 2, 2026 earnings release, filed with the SEC as an exhibit to the Form 8-K. Dollar amounts follow that release’s rounding. Fourth-quarter amounts are the company’s outlook.

How the figure is made

Implied upside is the average analyst price target minus the last regular-session close, divided by that close. Every public name uses that figure, and a higher number ranks first. A company that is not publicly traded stays on the list and is marked not rated, with no ticker, price, or score invented for it.

SpaceX was checked against its June 2026 Nasdaq listing. It trades as SPCX, so it is rated with the same figure as Apple, Amazon, NVIDIA, and Microsoft.

The names under consideration use that same figure and are not holdings. Google is scored as Alphabet Class A, ticker GOOGL. Class C, ticker GOOG, was checked and is not the share used here. Broadcom, Meta, and Eli Lilly were suggested for the seat they could fill. That suggestion is why they are on the list. Their order is still the upside figure. The Broadcom note beside that figure is a separate write-up of the company’s reported results.

Closes are the October 16, 2026, 4:00 p.m. ET regular session. Average targets were retrieved October 17, 2026. These numbers are a dated snapshot, not a live quote, and they are not investment advice.
